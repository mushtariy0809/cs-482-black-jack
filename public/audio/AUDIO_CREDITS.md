# Audio credits

Every sound in `public/audio/` is free to use. All of it is **CC0 1.0 (public domain)**:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Each sound effect is kept as the original `.ogg` plus an `.mp3` copy for browsers that can't play OGG.
The music was re-encoded smaller (OGG ~q3 and 96 kbps MP3). Nothing else was changed.
